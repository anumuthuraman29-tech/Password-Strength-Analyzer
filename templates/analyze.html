Understanding Password Strength Metrics

Weak (Score 0-2) Vulnerable to automated dictionary attacks and instant rainbow table lookups. Must not be used for any sensitive authentication.
Medium (Score 3-4) Resistant to basic automated bots, but remains susceptible to targeted GPU-accelerated hash cracking without multi-factor authentication.
Strong (Score 5) Meets NIST 800-63B and OWASP guidelines with multi-pool character distribution and sufficient brute-force calculation time.
